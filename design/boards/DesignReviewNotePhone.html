Drawing: how it works

• Rectangle, Ellipse and Line (the shape menu, or R, O, L) draw inside a board where you drag. The shape keeps that spot (X and Y) and size, filled surface with a line stroke, and saves to the board on your design branch.

• Text (T): click inside a board to place a text layer and type; Enter keeps it.

• Pen (P): a stroke with Apple Pencil, or a drag, inks a mark on the canvas: danger, W 2.5, the same width on screen at any zoom. Marks sit beside boards like sticky notes, never inside one, and save to canvas.json. Select one to set its rotation, size, opacity, stroke colour, W and Arrow end; drag it to move it; Delete removes it. Tapping points instead draws a vector in a board; Enter, or a tap on the first point, ends it.

• Frame (F): drag on empty canvas to make a new board (Web 1440 or Phone 390 by its width, named Board N until you rename it); inside a board it makes a frame layer.

• Sticky notes (S): click on the canvas; they save to canvas.json and show over the boards they point at. Layers lists a page's marks and sticky notes after its boards.

• Escape goes back to Move (V); the pen keeps inking until you do. Layers drawn in a board can be edited in the properties panel and undone with Reset.
