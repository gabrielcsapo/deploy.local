Ask from anywhere

Type what you want on any device. A question gets an answer. A change becomes a task on a computer that is free.

Review, then ship

Every change arrives as a pull request with its checks and screenshots you ask for. Nothing goes live until you press Ship.

Watched while it runs

Create an agent like Ops watcher and confirm its Looks after items. When it finds a problem, it asks you before acting.

On computers you already own.

One computer is enough to start. Add others and give each one a job. Models run where you choose: Claude or Codex with your own subscription, or an open model on your own GPU.
