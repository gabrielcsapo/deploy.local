Design system · rows

What a row holds sits under it.

A job and the files it kept: nested rows indent to the job’s text on a hairline from its glyph. Files are mono and truncate in the middle; a file not kept is muted with its reason. A command wraps instead of truncating. Phone rows show the web’s status glyphs.
