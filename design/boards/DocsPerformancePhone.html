Performance

Requests always has totals. Performance shows more when your app reports it: route timing, page experience and errors.

Route timing

Add a Server-Timing header to each response, naming the route pattern, not the address.

Server-Timing: route;desc="/photos/:id", match;dur=0.4, render;dur=38

route is required, once per response. match, render and action are optional milliseconds, the steps of the waterfall.

Page experience

From the page, POST a JSON batch to /__deploy/telemetry on the app's own address.
