The loop

From a signal to a verified release.

Twelve steps, three of them yours. Light steps run free on SER; coding and review use your subscriptions. Your steps start as "ask me" and loosen as trust builds.
01 · Local modelSignalA gap, failed check, app alert or your message
02 · Local modelTriageThe agent finds the project and what's known
03 · Local modelProposalWhat, why, and how it's tested
04 · YouYou approveStart it, make an issue, or dismiss
05 · SubscriptionTaskCodes on a computer, runs tests, saves a checkpoint
06 · SubscriptionPull requestAgent-written title and summary
07 · SubscriptionReviewA different model reviews; the author fixes
08 · YouMergeYou approve, or auto-merge once trusted
09 · YouReleasePrepare, verify, activate, roll back on failure
10 · Local modelVerifyWarden watches health after release
11 · Local modelReportBack in the conversation it came from
12 · Local modelLearnCorrections saved to the agent's instructions
