Working together

How the team plans, decides and hands work over. It is short on purpose: read it once, then come back to the part you need.

Planning

Work is planned in cycles. Before a cycle starts, each person writes down what they expect to finish and what could get in the way. The plan is a list, not a document: anyone should be able to read it in a minute.

  • Pick only the few things each person can finish.
  • Name the person who decides if the scope has to change.
  • Leave one afternoon free for what nobody planned.

A plan can change

Changing the plan is fine. Changing it without telling the others is not.

Roles and hours

Handing work over

A handover is a note, not a meeting. It says what is done, what is left and where to look first. The person receiving it replies once with what they understood.

What a note contains

  1. What is finished, in one sentence.
  2. What is not, and why it is not.
  3. The first thing to open, with a link.

Use handover in the title so the note can be found later. Questions go in the same thread, not in a new one.
