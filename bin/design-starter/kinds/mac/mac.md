## Mac kind: a window that looks like it belongs on macOS

A Mac board is one window: `<meta name="design-board" content="platform=mac,width=1280,height=800,schemes=light dark">`; width and height are the window's. Boards link `tokens.css`, `components.css` and `mac.css`, start with `.ds-mac-desk` around one `.ds-mac-window`. The check draws it once at its own size, light and dark. A board carries no `<style>`: `mac.css` sets the page's margin and ground. Each pane is a screen: the main window, the settings window, the menu-bar extra.

- **Type is macOS.** `--text-mac-body` 13 for everything you read and click, `--text-mac-small` 11 for secondary lines, section headers, counts and the subtitle, `--text-mac-headline` 15, `--text-mac-title` 20 and `--text-mac-large` 26 for a detail pane's title. Never 16 px body, never the web or phone scale.
- **The window.** `.ds-mac-lights` (three dots) at the left of `.ds-mac-titlebar`, then the title (and one `.ds-mac-subtitle`), then toolbar items (`.ds-mac-tool`, 28 px, icons only, a search field at the right). A unified 52 px bar; no second row of tabs under it.
- **Sidebar.** `.ds-mac-sidebar` with `.ds-mac-section` headers in 11 px and `.ds-mac-sideitem`s of 28 px; icons are in the accent, the current item is a grey fill (accent only with `.focused`, see Three panes). 220 px wide. The sidebar lists places or filters, never actions.
- **Three panes, at most.** Sidebar, then a `.ds-mac-list` (320 px) or a table, then the `.ds-mac-detail`; an `.ds-mac-inspector` (260 px) is a fourth only for properties of the selection. **Selection follows focus, as the system draws it: grey when its pane (or the window) does not have focus, the accent when it does.** A list or table row `.on` is the accent in the focused pane, which is the list unless it says `.unfocused`; a sidebar row `.on` is grey (a sidebar is rarely the focus) and the accent when the sidebar is `.focused`. Exactly one pane is accent at a time. A window that is not frontmost shows grey lights and every selection grey: `.ds-mac-window.inactive`. Never an accent row in two panes, never an accent selection in an inactive window.
- **Native controls.** `.ds-mac-popup` for a choice of several, `.ds-mac-seg` for two to four, `.ds-mac-check` for on or off, `.ds-mac-search` for search, `.ds-mac-btn` for push buttons (one `.default` at most per window or sheet), `.ds-mac-slider`. 22 px tall, not 32 px; no web select, toggle switch or big rounded button.
- **Tables are dense.** `.ds-mac-table` has 24 px rows, an 11 px header, numbers right-aligned, selection in the accent. A Mac list has more rows on screen than a web list; do not copy the 52 px rows.
- **Settings window.** About 640 wide, sized to its form, not 1280: a `.plain` title bar (no rule under it) holding the lights and the current tab's name, then `.ds-mac-tabs`, the window's toolbar of tabs, one `.ds-mac-tab` per pane with its icon above its label (three to six panes, the current in the accent and a fill), then `.ds-mac-settings-body` with a `.ds-mac-form`: labels right-aligned in a 160 px column, controls left-aligned, notes in 11 px under a control, `.full` on a rule that spans both columns. No Save button; settings apply when changed. Tabs are never inside the body and never a segmented control.
- **Form controls.** A column of on/off settings is `.ds-mac-checks` under one label: checkboxes 8 px apart, a `.note` indented 22 px under the box it explains. A multi-line value is `.ds-mac-textarea` (72 px, fills the control column). **Popup width rule:** a popup inside a `.ds-mac-form` is 200 px wide so a column of them lines up; elsewhere it is as wide as its longest choice and never under 140. A toolbar search field is `.ds-mac-search.wide` (200 px).
- **Menu-bar extra.** `.ds-mac-menubar` with one `.extra` highlighted while open, `.ds-mac-popover` (300 px) under it (`.has-popover` on the container, `.under-extra` on the popover: 6 px under the bar, 16 px from the right; a popover from a toolbar item is `.under-tool`, under the title bar, right-aligned; it never covers what opened it): a short header, rows of 28 px, a rule, then the quit row. A popover is for glancing and one or two actions, not a small app.
- **Sheets** (`.ds-mac-sheet` over `.ds-mac-dim`) attach to the window's top, 420 px wide, one task, Cancel at the left of the buttons and the `.default` at the right. A dialog that floats free is an alert, with an icon, a bold line and two buttons.
- **Light and dark** both, from tokens only. Corner radius 12 on the window, 6 on controls.

- **Sample content and product facts.** What a person would have in the app (note titles and text, folder names, a contact, a file's name and date) is drawn real, specific and plausible, with no flag: "Soup stock notes", "Recipes", "Edited yesterday". Never "Item one", "Secondary line" or "Item count": a window of filler is a wireframe. What the product does or decides (a shortcut, a setting's default, a sync interval, a policy, a price) is the brief's; when it isn't given, leave it out, or draw a plausible value and flag it with `data-question`. Worked example: the note "Soup stock notes" is sample; "Sync every 15 minutes" is a fact and carries the flag. The menu bar's clock is the system's. A reviewer lists each unflagged fact as a `[fact]` finding, and filler as one too.

What agents get wrong for the Mac:

- Accent selection in every pane at once, or in an inactive window.
- A settings window 1280 wide, tabs inside the body, or popups of three different widths in one form.
- A popover that covers its own menu-bar extra.

- A web app in a window: a 248 px sidebar with big rows, 15 px text, rounded pill buttons, a hero header.
- Controls that are too large and too round, and a toggle switch where the Mac has a checkbox.
- Putting tabs in the window instead of the toolbar, or buttons with text where an icon with a tooltip belongs.
- Colour everywhere. Mac uses grey and one accent: icons in the sidebar, selection, the checkbox when on.
- A single 1280 px pane filled with a form. The Mac shows structure: list and detail.
- Forgetting the inactive state, or drawing only light.
